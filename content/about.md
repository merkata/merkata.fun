### Tech

I am a Kubestronaut with plans to be a golden Kubestronaut next year.

I've been working with public cloud for the past 8 years.

Before that I worked in telco for 10+ years and participated in the great
transformation from OpenVMS/VAX machines through Red Hat clusters to VMWare VMs.

I enjoy writing in Go, Elixir and Ruby. I do write in Python as well. And shell scripts.

Currently I'm an individual contributor doing DevSecOps things in the cloud
with k8s. Full stack platform engineering with bells and whistles.

I've also been a team lead and an engineering manager on a couple of occasions.

I, for one, welcome our AI overlords.
