I am wrapping up the golden Kubestronaut certification prep for this year. So
far I have the kubestronaut certifications (KCNA, KCSA, CKA, CKAD, CKS)
followed by OTCA, PCA, CGOA, CAPA, ICA and CCA in this order and grouping of
two (thematically these are observability, gitops and networking). I did learn
a lot of new things and I am looking forward to the next/final batch of KCA,
CBA, CNPA, CNPE and LFCS. I am tempted to squeeze in the new CKNE there as
well. I prepared mostly by going over the KodeKloud materials (they have a
great golden Kubestronaut learning path), a practice lab that I am building and
the official documentation of every project.

By the end of year and before starting with the next batch, I want to expand
the lab and do more hands on stuff. Going through the documentation was
sufficient for the test based exams, but nothing beats actual practice. Looking
at my exam results, taking the time with the Argo ecosystem and playing with it
in my lab definitely paid off.

I also started to appreciate service meshes more and I really like that there
is a Gateway API now that networking projects are starting to implement.

December will mostly be getting more familiar with LLMs and agents, revisiting
and learning architectural patterns and system design, while I continue to play
around in my lab with what I have learned so far.
